# GSE47360 tissue-scope sensitivity (September 28, 2026)

Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE47360 . The old contrast pooled six endometriosis cases against three controls, but three cases are eutopic endometrial stromal cells and three are ectopic ovarian-cyst stromal cells. All controls are eutopic endometrial stromal cells. Nine retained full GEO records were SHA-256 checked against `../sources/GSE47360_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`; their probe tables were extracted directly. `gse47360_tissue_scope.py` computes Hedges g on the historical six-versus-three split and an exploratory three eutopic-case-versus-three eutopic-control split, on the 33,297 probes shared by every source sample.

Of 33,292 probes with finite effects in both comparisons, 6,838 reverse sign (1,965 have |g| >= 0.2 on both sides). This shows the old pooled effect is sensitive to inclusion of ectopic cells. Gene mapping, downstream meta and panel were **not** updated. Probe differences are correlated, so these counts are neither independent tests nor a test of clinical accuracy. The same-tissue group has just three cases and three controls; a direction change is not proof the ectopic tissue caused it, and culture, donor composition and batch may differ. This is post-exposure sensitivity, **not** an untouched validation, discovery, or published same-task benchmark win. Results in `gse47360_tissue_scope_probes.csv.gz`; summary in JSON. Reproduce with `python3 projects/endometriosis/sensitivity/gse47360_tissue_scope.py`; test with `python3 -m pytest -q projects/endometriosis/sensitivity/test_tissue_scope.py`.
