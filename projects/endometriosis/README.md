# Endometriosis - proposed separate project

Status: project scaffold and gate audit only, not a finished paper or a positive benchmark. The parent repository is shared core; this directory must hold a disease-specific protocol, accession and external-service evidence ledger, reproducible results and a 50-page substantive paper before its gates can be claimed.

Current disease-tagged manifest records: 120 = 14 GSE identifiers + 105 nested GSM samples + 1 other. These are record units, not independent datasets or patients. The shared 40-service and 49-page PDF do not transfer as automatic per-project passes. Benchmark/discovery endpoint is open.

Disease-specific documents and source logs are not yet split from shared core; use the shared source code and result filenames by disease as leads, then verify original record attribution before copying.

## GSE7846 tissue-label contradiction

The historically used [GSE7846](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE7846) discovery series contains five case and five control arrays. Its series title, study summary and all sample **titles** say cultured human endometrial endothelial cells (HEECs) from eutopic endometrium. But individual full GEO records have a systematic split: all five case **descriptions** say "Gene expression data from ectopic endometrium," while all five control descriptions say eutopic. Individual characteristics name endothelial cells from a patient with endometriosis or a normal control, without resolving the anatomical source. `sources/GSE7846_description_conflict.csv` preserves every fetched URL, response hash and competing fields. Thus case/control diagnosis is source-grounded, but the case tissue's eutopic-versus-ectopic identity is not, and this series must not support a lesion-origin versus eutopic design claim. It remains a previously analyzed record-level cohort with disclosed uncertainty; the ten individually fetched, historically analyzed GSMs are now present as provenance records in the disease manifest, without settling that tissue-label conflict. A depositor clarification or patient-to-site record is needed before any anatomical inference. The affected endpoint stays open.

## Ten previously used GSE7846 specimen records reconciled

The ten [GSE7846](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE7846) GSMs were already used in the retained discovery expression/label series (`results/series/endometriosis__GSE7846.labels.csv`, five cases and five controls). We re-fetched all ten full source records on September 27; byte hashes match the prior title/description/characteristic/label crosswalk in `sources/GSE7846_description_conflict.csv`, and gzip copies of the individual source records are in `sources/GSE7846/`, losslessly decompressible and rehashable against the crosswalk. The ten now count as **nested, historically used specimen identifiers**, moving this disease inventory from 101 to 111 distinct records (14 GSE, 96 GSM, one ontology). This is not ten new studies or a new expression run. Case tissue is still anatomically unresolved: titles/series state eutopic endometrial endothelial cells, but all five case descriptions say ectopic. No lesion-origin validation, clinical marker, fair published benchmark or external service is gained. The record-unit floor is still nine short; the independent science and paper gates remain open.

## GSE47360 mixed tissue in the old case arm

All nine historically used [GSE47360](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE47360) GSMs were freshly source-fetched; `sources/GSE47360_used_sample_crosswalk.csv` holds exact URLs, hashes, title, tissue, cell-type and disease fields; lossless gzip copies of the full source records are under `sources/GSE47360/`. The saved historical label file has three controls and six cases. The source's three controls are eutopic endometrial stromal cells from non-endometriosis donors; among six cases, **three are eutopic endometrial stromal cells from endometriosis donors and three are ectopic ovarian chocolate-cyst stromal cells**. The old pooled 6-vs-3 effect therefore mixes diagnosis with anatomical origin and cannot be reported as either a pure eutopic same-tissue case/control comparison or a pure lesion-origin marker. The original effect and downstream meta are retained as historical mixed contrasts, not silently corrected. Donor pairing across the two case tissue groups is not established by the source titles. No new panel or favorable reanalysis is claimed.

The nine already used, source-verified nested GSM identifiers are now in the disease manifest, raising **111 to 120 unique accession-record units** (14 GSE, 105 GSM, one ontology). This meets only the literal record-unit floor, not 120 independent studies or donors. The 40-service, disease-specific 50-page Times paper, actual discovery and fair published same-task benchmark gates remain unmet. GSE7846's separate ectopic/eutopic description conflict remains unresolved as well.

### GSE47360 same-tissue audit (September 28)

A source-record probe-level sensitivity now compares the historical six mixed-site cases against the three controls with a **post-exposure** restriction to the three eutopic cases versus the same three eutopic controls. It SHA-256 checks all nine records, uses 33,297 common probes and finds 6,838 effect-sign changes among 33,292 finite comparisons (1,965 changes have |g| >= 0.2 on both sides). This demonstrates instability to tissue restriction, not a causal tissue attribution or a new marker. Only three source cases survive the restriction, and the gene-level meta and panel were not favorably refit. See `sensitivity/README.md` for the method, full results, and caveats.
